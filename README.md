# 🎯 The Boise Gun Club
### *Building Treasure Valley's First Comprehensive Firearms Community Platform*

## 🏔️ **Connecting Idaho's Gun Heritage**

**The Boise Gun Club** isn't just another website—it's the **digital heartland** for Idaho's 250,000+ gun owners across the Treasure Valley. We're building what should have existed from day one: a unified, professional platform that brings together every piece of our fragmented firearms community.

### **🎪 One Platform to Unite It All**

- **🏢 117+ Verified Businesses** - From FFLs to ranges, gunsmiths to trainers
- **📅 Smart Event Coordination** - Never miss another match, training, or gun show
- **🌤️ Range Intel Dashboard** - Real-time weather conditions for optimal shooting
- **⭐ Community Reviews** - Honest feedback from fellow Idaho shooters
- **🛡️ Safety-First Culture** - Responsible firearms education and awareness
