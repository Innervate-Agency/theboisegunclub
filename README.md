# 🎯 The Boise Gun Club
### *Your Digital Home for Treasure Valley Gun Owners*

---

**The Boise Gun Club** is where Idaho gun owners connect, share knowledge, and support local businesses. Everything you need for the shooting sports in one place.

## What You'll Find Here

- **📅 Events**: Never miss a match, gun show, or training class in the Treasure Valley
- **🏢 Local Businesses**: Find trusted FFLs, ranges, trainers, and gunsmiths near you
- **📚 Knowledge**: Idaho-specific guides for gun laws, safety, and best practices
- **🗺️ Shooting Locations**: Real conditions at BLM and Forest Service shooting areas
- **🛒 Deals**: Local inventory and deals from Treasure Valley gun stores
- **💬 Community**: Connect with fellow Idaho gun owners who share your interests

## Why We Built This

Idaho gun owners deserve better than scattered Facebook groups and outdated websites. We're building a single place where you can:

- Find reliable local businesses
- Stay informed about events and legal changes
- Share knowledge with fellow shooters
- Support the Treasure Valley firearms community

Built by Idaho gun owners, for Idaho gun owners.
