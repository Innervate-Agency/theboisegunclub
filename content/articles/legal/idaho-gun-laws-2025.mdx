# Idaho Gun Laws: Complete 2025 Guide

Idaho has some of the most firearms-friendly laws in the United States. As a constitutional carry state, Idaho recognizes your Second Amendment rights while maintaining reasonable regulations for public safety.

## Constitutional Carry in Idaho

Idaho became a constitutional carry state in 2016, meaning that law-abiding citizens who are 18 years or older can carry a concealed handgun without a permit. However, there are important details to understand:

### Who Can Carry

- **Age 18+**: Idaho residents can carry concealed without a permit
- **Non-residents**: Must have a valid concealed carry permit from their home state
- **Federal restrictions**: Still apply (felons, domestic violence convictions, etc.)

### Where You Can Carry

Idaho's constitutional carry applies in most public places, but there are important exceptions:

- **Courthouses**: Prohibited except for law enforcement
- **Schools**: K-12 school grounds are off-limits
- **Federal buildings**: Post offices, federal courthouses, etc.
- **Private property**: Property owners can restrict carry

## Enhanced Concealed Carry License

While not required for basic carry, Idaho's Enhanced Concealed Carry License offers significant benefits:

### Benefits of the Enhanced License

1. **Reciprocity**: Recognized by 40+ states
2. **School zones**: Allows carry in federal gun-free school zones
3. **Faster purchases**: Skip background check delays
4. **Training certification**: Demonstrates formal training

### Application Process

The enhanced license requires:

- **Age**: 21 years minimum
- **Training**: NRA-certified course or equivalent
- **Background check**: FBI and state criminal history
- **Residency**: 6 months in Idaho
- **Fee**: $20 for residents

## Prohibited Locations

Even with constitutional carry, certain locations remain off-limits:

### State Prohibited Areas

- Courthouses and courtrooms
- Juvenile detention facilities
- Public schools (K-12)
- Secure areas of airports

### Federal Prohibited Areas

- Post offices
- Federal buildings
- Military installations
- National parks (some exceptions apply)

## Purchase and Transfer Laws

Idaho follows federal firearms laws with minimal additional restrictions:

### Background Checks

- **Dealers**: Federal background check required
- **Private sales**: No background check required between residents
- **Gun shows**: Same rules apply

### Age Requirements

- **Handguns**: 21 years for dealer purchases, 18+ for private sales
- **Long guns**: 18 years for all purchases

## Transportation Rules

Idaho has straightforward transportation laws:

### Vehicle Carry

- **Loaded firearms**: Legal in vehicles with constitutional carry
- **Concealment**: Not required in vehicles
- **Accessibility**: Driver and passengers may access firearms

### Travel Considerations

- **Other states**: Know their laws when traveling
- **Reciprocity**: Enhanced license provides better recognition
- **Federal property**: Special rules may apply

## Recent Legislative Updates (2024-2025)

### New Developments

- **Enhanced reciprocity**: Additional states now recognize Idaho permits
- **Campus carry**: Limited expansion for permit holders
- **Constitutional carry expansion**: Age reduction discussions ongoing
